About Me

Welcome. I am Barbara Franca, a licensed psychologist dedicated to helping individuals navigate life's challenges, resolve emotional difficulties, and foster meaningful personal growth.

My therapeutic approach is empathetic, collaborative, and tailored to your individual needs. I integrate evidence-based modalities—such as Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), mindfulness practices, and psychodynamic insight—to create a compassionate environment where you can speak freely and work toward sustainable change.

Qualifications

  • Licensed Psychologist
  • Master's in Clinical Psychology
  • Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT)
  • Mindfulness-Based Practices
  • Confidential Online & In-Person Care

Areas of Practice
