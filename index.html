About Me

Welcome. As a Psychologist and Specialist in Neuropsychology, my practice focuses on understanding the intricate relationship between brain function, cognition, and behavior. I provide evidence-based evaluations and therapeutic interventions tailored to help individuals navigate cognitive, emotional, and psychological challenges.

Areas of Expertise

Neuropsychological Assessment

Comprehensive evaluations to assess memory, attention, executive functioning, and cognitive strengths or deficits.

Cognitive Rehabilitation

Targeted therapeutic strategies designed to rebuild or compensate for cognitive functions affected by neurological conditions or stress.

Clinical Psychotherapy

Individualized psychological support aimed at fostering resilience, emotional regulation, and self-understanding.
